# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

LIFE//FORENSICS is a project for people who want to build, learn, experiment, and share ideas.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Welcome different levels of experience.
- Give and receive technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and decisions rather than people.
- Respect privacy and intellectual property.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples include:

- Harassment or threats.
- Discriminatory or hateful language.
- Personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately exposing credentials or sensitive datasets.
- Trolling or repeated bad-faith disruption.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer privately with relevant details.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive and safe.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and interactions directly related to LIFE//FORENSICS.
