# LIFE//FORENSICS Demo Guide

This guide provides a short demonstration flow for hackathons, interviews, and portfolio reviews.

## 1. Start With the Problem

Use:

> Personal data is everywhere, but its story is fragmented.

Explain that music, transactions, locations, timestamps, and other signals often live in separate systems.

## 2. Introduce the Solution

> LIFE//FORENSICS connects fragmented signals and turns them into explainable discoveries.

## 3. Show the Dataset

Current documented prototype metrics:

```text
18,133 normalized records
7,413 music signals
10,720 transaction signals
3,017 active days
1,209 location strings
2013–2024 coverage
```

## 4. Open the Investigation Console

Show how supported investigation questions map to measurable evidence.

Focus on the transition:

```text
Question
   ↓
Query
   ↓
Evidence
   ↓
Discovery
```

## 5. Open a Discovery

Show:

- Discovery title.
- Evidence.
- Confidence.
- Reasoning.
- Contributing sources.
- Limitation.

Emphasize that the system does not simply make an unexplained claim.

## 6. Explore the Life Graph

Show relationships between:

- Time.
- Music.
- Transactions.
- Locations.
- Recurring entities.

## 7. Explore the Timeline

Show how activity changes across time and where activity clusters appear.

## 8. Explain Privacy

Show that unnecessary identifying fields are excluded before behavioral analysis.

## 9. Close With the Core Idea

> LIFE//FORENSICS doesn't simply visualize data. It reconstructs patterns from fragmented signals and explains discoveries with evidence.

## Demo Notes

Keep the demonstration focused on one clear investigation story rather than trying to show every feature.

Avoid presenting correlation as causation and explain uncertainty when discussing anomaly candidates.
